# Multi-System Homeopathic Clinical Framework & AI Evaluation Hub

## 🩺 Overview
This repository showcases a structured collection of multi-system clinical case studies, repertorial analysis methodologies, and AI prompt engineering rubrics. Drawing from active clinical homeopathic practice since 2016, this project bridges traditional medical case management with modern Artificial Intelligence (AI) evaluation, focusing on data annotation, prompt optimization, and domain-specific safety guidelines.

## 🌟 Key Focus Areas
* **Multi-System Clinical Practice:** Documenting and structuring diverse health conditions spanning dermatological, respiratory, gastrointestinal, musculoskeletal, and chronic constitutional disorders.
* **Repertorization & Symptom Totality:** Applying classical homeopathic principles (totality of symptoms, physical/mental modalities, and repertorial mapping) to structured data formats.
* **AI Domain Expertise & Prompt Engineering:** Designing rigorous prompt templates and evaluation metrics to test Large Language Models (LLMs) on medical reasoning, edge cases, and safety compliance.

## 📂 Repository Structure
- `datasets/` : Anonymized multi-system clinical case templates and symptom mappings (JSON/CSV).
- `prompts/` : Specialized prompt engineering templates designed for medical domain testing and fine-tuning.
- `evaluation_rubrics/` : Quality assurance metrics for assessing AI-generated medical explanations and safety boundaries.
